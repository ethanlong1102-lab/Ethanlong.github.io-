BEFORE YOU BOOK

Questions we get asked.

Is the call really free?+

Yes. Thirty minutes, no card, no obligation. If it turns out we're not the right studio for the job, we'll tell you who is.

Why don't you publish prices?+

Because tiers are guesses. A two-hour brand shoot and a three-day wedding don't belong on the same menu. We scope once, after we understand the work.

What should I bring?+

Rough dates, rough budget, and anything that shows the feeling you're after — a reel, a mood board, a competitor's site you envy.

Can you take just one piece — say, only photo?+

Usually we work as one package, because that's what keeps a brand consistent. Tell us on the call and we'll be straight with you about whether a single craft makes sense.
